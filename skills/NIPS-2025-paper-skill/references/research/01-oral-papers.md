# NeurIPS 2025 Dataset Track - Oral Papers (7 篇)

## Best Paper: Artificial Hivemind
- **ID**: 121421 | **OpenReview**: saDOrrnNTz
- **标题**: Artificial Hivemind: The Open-Ended Homogeneity of Language Models (and Beyond)
- **作者**: Liwei Jiang, Yuanjun Chai, Margaret Li, Mickel Liu, Raymond Fok, Nouha Dziri, Yulia Tsvetkov, Maarten Sap, Yejin Choi
- **核心**: 引入 Infinity-Chat (26K queries + 31,250 human annotations)，提出 6 类 17 子类 prompt 类型分类，揭示 LM mode collapse 和模型间同质性

## Oral 2: WebGen-Bench
- **ID**: 121451 | **OpenReview**: q2VpjD7k1V
- **标题**: WebGen-Bench: Evaluating LLMs on Generating Interactive and Functional Websites from Scratch
- **作者**: Zimu Lu, Yunqiao Yang et al.
- **核心**: 647 个测试用例，13 个子类别，最强配置仅 27.8% 准确率，附带训练集 WebGen-Instruct (6,667 条)

## Oral 3: BEDLAM2.0
- **ID**: 121502 | **OpenReview**: ii9kVwf95a
- **标题**: BEDLAM2.0: Synthetic humans and cameras in motion
- **作者**: Joachim Tesch, Giorgio Becherini et al. (MPI)
- **核心**: 扩展 BEDLAM 数据集，增强相机/身体/服装多样性，显著提升世界坐标运动估计性能

## Oral 4: CoralVQA
- **ID**: 121508 | **OpenReview**: iRsZHAMNHL
- **标题**: CoralVQA: A Large-Scale Visual Question Answering Dataset for Coral Reef Image Understanding
- **作者**: Hongyong Han, Wei Wang et al.
- **核心**: 12,805 images, 67 genera, 3 oceans, 277,653 QA pairs，与海洋生物学家合作的半自动构建流程

## Oral 5: OrthoLoC
- **ID**: 121611 | **OpenReview**: VcUmuGXhaI | **ArXiv**: 2509.18350
- **标题**: OrthoLoC: UAV 6-DoF Localization and Calibration Using Orthographic Geodata
- **作者**: Oussema Dhaouadi et al.
- **核心**: 16,425 multi-modal UAV images，AdHoP 技术提升匹配性能达 95%

## Oral 6: RISEBench (Envisioning Beyond the Pixels)
- **ID**: 121633 | **OpenReview**: T3UksaPK64 | **ArXiv**: 2504.02826
- **标题**: Envisioning Beyond the Pixels: Benchmarking Reasoning-Informed Visual Editing
- **作者**: Xiangyu Zhao et al.
- **核心**: 首个推理驱动视觉编辑基准，4 种推理类别 360 样本，最强 GPT-image-1 仅 28.8%

## Oral 7: NOVA
- **ID**: 121770 | **OpenReview**: DwFDfrPsm8 | **ArXiv**: 2505.14064
- **标题**: NOVA: A Benchmark for Rare Anomaly Localization and Clinical Reasoning in Brain MRI
- **作者**: Cosmin Bercea et al.
- **核心**: ~900 brain MRI, 281 种罕见病理，三维评估（定位/描述/推理），VLM 性能骤降 40-65%
